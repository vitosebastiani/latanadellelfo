Our story

A trattoria built to protect Bari, not imitate it.

Between handmade pasta, seafood from the Apulian coast and sincere hospitality, La Locanda dell'Elfo keeps defending a cuisine made of real gestures, clear flavors and ingredients that do not need disguises.

Lunch is relaxed, dinner is more atmospheric, and every table should leave with the same feeling: they ate well, in a place with its own identity.
